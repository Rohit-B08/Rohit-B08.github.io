About

From backend code to production support

Computer Science graduate with three co-ops of hands-on development and testing experience.

I'm a recent Computer Science graduate from the University of New Brunswick, with hands-on software development experience across backend systems, QA, and Agile delivery.

I built backend and data-driven solutions in .NET, Visual Basic, and SQL as an IT Student at NB Power, including incident management and root-cause analysis on production issues, and automation workflows using Microsoft Power Platform. At SpryPoint, I worked across the frontend and backend of a live SaaS product, writing JUnit tests and optimizing PostgreSQL queries through Play Framework. At Cvent, I worked as an SDET inside an Agile team, participating in scrum ceremonies and partnering directly with product owners and stakeholders.

Alongside my co-ops, I spent three years in leadership with the UNB Cybersecurity Club — first as Treasurer, then as Vice President — competing in CTF events like HackFest and helping run a community of student security enthusiasts.

I'm now focused on backend development and application support roles, where the incident-response and cross-team troubleshooting instincts I built at NB Power meet the hands-on coding I've done throughout my degree.
